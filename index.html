Your path to United starts on campus.

Campus ambassadors are students who work with United on internships, recruiting and events at their own school. Find yours, book some time, and see what’s coming up this semester.

Step 1

Pick your campus

Your choice filters the ambassador directory, the events board and the meeting request form below. Every campus has two ambassadors.

Not at one of these four? We’re still here for you — pick the campus closest to you and book time. Every ambassador is happy to meet virtually.

Step 2

Your ambassadors

Reach out directly, or use the request form to send a meeting time.

Step 3

Request a meeting

Fill this in and it drafts the email for you — send it, or copy the text if your mail client doesn’t open.

About the program

        
What ambassadors actually do

      

      

        

          

            
          

          
Connect students to recruiters

          
We know which roles open when, and who to send your resume to instead of
             dropping it into a portal.

        

        

          

            
          

          
Run events on campus

          
Info sessions, pilot and ops Q&As, resume reviews, and partnerships with
             student orgs that want an airline in the room.

        

        

          

            
          

          
Answer the awkward questions

          
Standby travel, what a first-year actually does, whether your major matters.
             Ask us instead of guessing.

        

      


      

        Do I have to be a business or aviation major?
        
No. The teams that hire from campus span technology, operations, finance,
           marketing, data, safety and customer experience. Ambassadors across these four
           campuses come from different majors on purpose.

      

      

        Is this only for juniors and seniors?
        
No. First and second years are the people who benefit most from talking early,
           because internship timelines start earlier than most students expect.

      

      

        What if my school isn’t one of the four?
        
You’re still welcome. Pick the nearest campus and book time — every
           ambassador meets virtually, and can point you to the general careers site and to
           events that are open to any student.
